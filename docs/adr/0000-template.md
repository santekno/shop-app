# NNNN. Short title in the imperative ("Use X for Y")

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN (link to the newer ADR)
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Chapter: Bab N

## Context

What problem are we solving, and what forces are at play (requirements, constraints, team skills, cost)? Facts only, no decision yet.

## Decision

What we decided, in one or two sentences, starting with "We will …". Then the details that matter.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — why not.
- **Option B** — why not.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we must now do or watch. Include the negative consequences.
